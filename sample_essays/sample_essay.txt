Have you ever wondered whether learning at home could be better than sitting in a classroom?
I believe online education is better than traditional classroom learning because it is easy.

Online classes are convenient. Students can learn from home. Many people like this a lot and it is very good for everyone.
For example, a 2020 report by a national education agency found that about one third of college students worked at least 20 hours a week.
Some people say that online learning makes students feel lonely.
However, students can join video calls and discussion boards, so they can still talk with classmates.

In conclusion, online education is a good choice for students.
